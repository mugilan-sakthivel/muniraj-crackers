# Client Guide — Muniraj Crackers Website Content

Manage products, prices and categories yourself. No code needed.
Changes appear on the website automatically within ~10 minutes.

## Open the Studio

1. Go to <https://muniraj-crackers-8bk97zyk.sanity.studio>
2. Sign in with the Sanity account you were invited with.
3. You will see **Category**, **Product**, **Site settings** and other lists.

## Change a price

1. Open **Product** and click the product name.
2. Edit **Estimate price (paise)** — this is the offer price customers see.
   - Prices are in paise: ₹100 = `10000`. (A ₹45.50 pack = `4550`.)
   - **List price (paise)** is the MRP shown struck through.
3. Click **Publish**. The website updates within ~10 minutes.

## Hide a product (out of stock / seasonal)

1. Open the product, turn **Available for enquiry** OFF, Publish.
2. It disappears from the website instantly-ish (within ~10 minutes).
3. To bring it back, turn it back ON. Nothing is deleted.

## Add a new product

1. Open **Product**, click **Create** (top right).
2. Fill in: Product name, SKU (any unique code), Category (pick from list),
   Pack content (e.g. "10 pcs box"), List price + Estimate price in paise,
   Sort order (a number — lower shows first).
3. Click **Generate** next to Slug so the web address is created.
4. Upload a **Primary image** (optional but recommended).
5. Click **Publish**.

## Remove a product permanently

Open the product → click the three dots menu (top right) → **Delete**.
Prefer hiding (above) unless you are sure.

## Add / hide a category

Same idea under **Category**: create new ones, or turn **Visible on website** OFF
to hide a whole category and its products.

## See customer enquiries

When WhatsApp enquiry tracking is enabled, every time a customer taps “Open
WhatsApp”, their name, mobile and list is saved under **WhatsApp enquiry
intents** (newest first). Use it for follow-up.

## Rules that protect the website

- Never leave Product name, SKU, Category, Pack content or prices empty.
- Prices must be whole numbers in paise (no decimals, no ₹ symbol).
- If something looks wrong on the website after an edit, check the field above
  first — 99% of issues are a missing price or category.

Questions? Contact your developer with the product name and what you changed.
